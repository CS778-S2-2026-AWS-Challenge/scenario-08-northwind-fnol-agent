# Northwind Contents Standard Policy

This is synthetic wording for an MVP demonstration. It is not a real insurance contract. It must
be read with the matching Northwind policy schedule. The schedule identifies the insured address,
occupancy, contents sum insured, excesses, category sublimits, specified items, optional benefits,
endorsements, and period of insurance. The Agent must not use this wording as evidence of a
customer's cover unless the schedule and effective version match.

## CON-DEF-01 - Key terms

- **Contents** means movable household possessions owned, hired, or legally cared for by an insured
  person and normally kept at the insured address, subject to category exclusions and sublimits.
- **Accident** means a sudden and unintended event.
- **Event** means one occurrence, or a series of occurrences with one originating cause.
- **Loss** means accidental physical damage, destruction, or theft.
- **Specified item** means an item individually described in the schedule with its own limit.
- **Excess** means the part of an accepted claim the policyholder is responsible for, as shown in
  the policy schedule.

Motor vehicles, animals, land, buildings, business stock, and property held mainly for sale are not
contents under this synthetic policy unless an endorsement expressly includes them.

## CON-COV-01 - Accidental loss to contents

Contents Standard includes cover for accidental loss to contents at the insured address during the
period of insurance, including fire, storm, flood, escape of water, impact, malicious damage, and
theft, subject to the schedule, exclusions, responsibilities, excesses, and limits.

The existence of this section does not prove that an individual item or event is covered. Ownership,
location, cause, category, item limit, security conditions, and endorsements may need confirmation.

## CON-COV-02 - Theft

Theft may be covered when it occurs at the insured address or another location expressly allowed by
this policy or the schedule. Theft must be reported to Police as soon as reasonably possible. The
claimant should provide the Police reference when issued and available evidence of ownership and
loss. Theft by an insured person or a person acting with that person's consent is excluded.

For theft from an unattended vehicle, storage facility, temporary accommodation, or another place
away from home, visible forced entry, reasonable security, and location-specific limits may apply.
Unclear circumstances require professional review.

## CON-BEN-01 - Temporary accommodation

If covered loss to the insured home makes it uninhabitable, reasonable temporary accommodation for
the household and domestic pets may be considered up to the limit and period in the contents
schedule. The benefit ends when the home is reasonably habitable, a permanent move occurs, the
claim is settled, or the limit is reached. Duplicate entitlement under another policy must be
disclosed and reviewed.

## CON-BEN-02 - Moving, temporary removal, and storage

Limited cover may apply while contents are being moved to a new permanent home, temporarily kept at
an approved location, or stored with a commercial storage provider. The permitted period, causes of
loss, territorial limits, security requirements, and maximum payment come from the schedule or
endorsement. The Agent must not treat ordinary worldwide or indefinite away-from-home cover as
included.

## CON-BEN-03 - Hidden gradual water-damage endorsement

Gradual damage is generally excluded. A limited contribution may be considered only when the
matching schedule includes the `hidden_water_damage` endorsement and a hidden internal leak or
overflow causes damage that was not visible, obvious, known, or reasonably discoverable earlier.
The endorsement limit and excess come from the schedule and applicability requires staff review.

## CON-LIM-01 - Sum insured, specified items, and category sublimits

The contents sum insured is the overall ceiling for ordinary contents loss from one event, subject
to settlement terms. Jewellery, watches, artworks, collections, bicycles, portable electronics,
cash, business tools, and other listed categories may have lower sublimits. A specified item uses
the item limit in the schedule. The Agent may retrieve recorded limits but must not infer an item's
category, value, or final payable amount when the evidence is incomplete.

## CON-CLM-01 - What to do after contents loss

The claimant should first protect people from immediate danger. As soon as reasonably possible, the
claimant should:

1. take safe and reasonable steps to prevent further loss;
2. report theft, burglary, vandalism, or another legally reportable event to Police;
3. notify Northwind and describe what happened, when, where, and which items were affected;
4. keep damaged items for reasonable inspection unless safety requires disposal;
5. retain photographs, receipts, valuations, serial numbers, warranty records, account records, or
   other available proof of ownership and value; and
6. tell Northwind if lost or stolen property is recovered.

Receipts are useful but are not declared the only possible evidence. Missing or not-yet-issued
evidence may be recorded as pending and supplied later.

## CON-CLM-02 - Information and cooperation

The claimant must provide honest and reasonably complete information and cooperate with authorised
inspection, assessment, replacement, recovery, or liability steps. The claimant must not admit
liability or settle a third-party demand on Northwind's behalf. Unknown details must remain unknown
rather than be invented.

## CON-EXC-01 - Excesses

The base excess and any theft, unoccupied-home, item, or endorsement excess come only from the
matching schedule. An excess normally applies to each event. A benefit may be excess-free only when
this wording or the schedule says so. The Agent may explain a recorded amount but multiple-event,
multiple-policy, additional-excess, or waiver questions require staff review.

## CON-SET-01 - How a contents claim may be settled

After assessment, Northwind may repair an item, replace it with a reasonably equivalent item, issue
a supplier credit, or pay a cash amount using the settlement basis in the schedule. Existing wear,
condition, age, depreciation where applicable, item and category limits, salvage, and proof of value
may affect settlement. Undamaged parts of a pair, set, collection, room, or matching group are not
automatically replaced. The Agent must not promise a method or amount.

## CON-EXC-02 - Wear, breakdown, and gradual causes

The policy does not cover ordinary wear and tear, deterioration, corrosion, rot, mould, mildew,
mechanical or electrical breakdown, or other gradual causes. Section CON-BEN-03 is the only
synthetic MVP exception and applies only with the matching endorsement.

## CON-EXC-03 - Defects, cleaning, and restoration

The policy does not cover the cost of correcting defective design, materials, workmanship, or an
unsuitable cleaning or restoration method. Separate resulting accidental damage may require
professional assessment.

## CON-EXC-04 - Unsecured or excluded property

There is no cover for unexplained disappearance without evidence of an event, property left in an
unlocked and unattended vehicle where reasonable security was available, business stock, illegal
property, or property not owned, hired, or legally cared for by an insured person. Location and
security disputes require staff review.

## CON-EXC-05 - Intentional loss and general exclusions

The policy does not cover intentional or reckless loss caused by an insured person, fines or
penalties, punitive damages, nuclear risk, war, terrorism, or a payment prohibited by sanctions.

## CON-AUTH-01 - Agent and staff authority

The Agent may explain this wording, collect FNOL facts, identify missing schedule or evidence data,
and cite exact sections. It must transfer coverage applicability, item classification disputes,
claim approval or rejection, liability, disputed excess, and settlement or payment decisions to
authorised staff.
