## Purpose of the Research

We are conducting this research as part of a University of Auckland course project to better understand people's experiences, challenges, and expectations when making an insurance claim, particularly during the First Notice of Loss (FNOL) and early claim-handling stages.

The research aims to identify common user needs, pain points, behaviours, and opportunities for improving the insurance claim experience.

## Who Can Participate

This research is intended primarily for New Zealand insurance customers and potential insurance claimants.

You may participate whether or not you have previously made an insurance claim.

## What Participation Involves

If you choose to participate, you will be asked to complete a short online survey about insurance claim experiences, challenges, expectations, and attitudes towards digital or AI-assisted claim services.

The survey is expected to take approximately **3–5 minutes**.

There is no payment, reward, or other compensation for participating in this research.

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is entirely voluntary.

You may choose not to participate without providing a reason. You may also stop completing the survey at any time before submitting your response, without penalty or disadvantage.

Because responses are intended to be anonymous, once a response has been submitted, we may not be able to identify and remove an individual response.

## Privacy and Anonymity

The survey is designed not to collect directly identifying information.

Please do **not** provide any personal, confidential, sensitive, or identifying information in your responses, including:

- your name;
- email address;
- phone number;
- insurance policy number;
- insurance claim number;
- vehicle registration number or licence plate;
- home or postal address; or
- any information that could identify you or another person.

Survey findings will be analysed and presented in aggregated or anonymised form.

Individual participants will not be identified in project outputs.

## How the Information Will Be Used

The information collected will be used only for this university course project and related academic activities.

The data may be used for:

- research analysis;
- aggregated statistics and visualisations;
- research dashboards;
- pain-point analysis;
- user personas;
- user journey maps;
- project documentation;
- project reports; and
- project presentations.

## Data Storage and Access

Survey responses will be collected and stored using Google Forms.

Access to the raw survey data will be limited to members of the **Second Breakfast Fellowship** project team.

The data will not be used for commercial purposes.

## Data Deletion

Raw survey data will be deleted after the course ends on **06/11/2026**.

## Potential Risks

This research is expected to involve minimal risk.

Some questions may ask you to recall frustrating, stressful, or negative insurance claim experiences. If you feel uncomfortable, you may stop completing the survey at any time before submitting your response.

## Consent

By choosing to continue with the survey, you confirm that:

- you have read and understood the information above;
- you understand the purpose of the research and what participation involves;
- you understand that participation is voluntary;
- you understand that you may stop before submitting your response;
- you understand how your anonymised responses may be used; and
- you voluntarily agree to participate in this research.
