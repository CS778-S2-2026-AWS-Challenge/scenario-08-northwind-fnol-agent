    Complete the professional review Three guided steps

    How this works: review every file the claimant supplied, resolve any internal review signal, then record the outcome and a safe customer update. Your internal reasoning stays private.

    1

    Review the claimant's existing evidence

    Work through every file supplied for this claim. Choose how credible and usable it is, then record the reason for your judgement. The AI credibility tag is reserved for a future agent assessment and does not make the decision.

    2

    Resolve internal review signals

    A signal is a question raised by the system, not a claim decision or fraud finding. Choose the signal, explain your finding, then confirm, dismiss, or resolve it.

    Select each question and save its finding before submitting.

    3

    Record the outcome and move the claim

    The evidence findings above determine whether the claim can continue or must wait. Record the internal outcome, then provide a separate claimant-safe update.

    Start this review before saving its final outcome.

    Privacy check: the internal result and reason codes stay in the staff record. Only the update immediately above is shown to the claimant.
