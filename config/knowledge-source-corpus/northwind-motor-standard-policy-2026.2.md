# Northwind Motor Standard Policy

This is synthetic policy wording. It is not a real insurance contract. It must
be read with the matching Northwind policy schedule. The schedule identifies the insured vehicle,
cover option, period of insurance, insured value, excesses, limits, named drivers, permitted use,
and endorsements. If the schedule is missing or does not match this document and its effective
period, the Agent must state the limitation and request staff review.

## MTR-DEF-01 - Key terms

- **Accident** means a sudden and unintended event.
- **Event** means one occurrence, or a series of occurrences with one originating cause.
- **Insured vehicle** means the vehicle identified in the matching policy schedule.
- **Loss** means accidental physical damage, destruction, or theft.
- **Excess** means the part of an accepted claim the policyholder is responsible for, as shown in
  the policy schedule.
- **Period of insurance** means the effective period shown in the matching policy schedule.

## MTR-COV-01 - Collision, fire, theft, storm, flood and accidental vehicle damage

Motor Standard includes cover for accidental loss to the insured vehicle when the event occurs in
New Zealand during the period of insurance. This includes collision, impact, fire, theft, and
malicious damage, subject to the schedule, exclusions, claim responsibilities, excesses, and
limits.

Storm, hail, flood and impact by falling objects are examples of accidental loss; deliberate
exposure to danger and the exclusions still require assessment.

The existence of this section does not prove that a particular event is covered. The exact vehicle,
driver, use, effective date, event facts, exclusions, and endorsements must be checked.

## MTR-COV-02 - Third-party property liability

The policy may respond when an authorised driver becomes legally liable for accidental physical
damage to another person's property arising from use of the insured vehicle in New Zealand during
the period of insurance. The maximum amount is the third-party liability limit in the schedule.

Northwind must control the defence and settlement of a third-party demand. The policyholder or
driver must not admit liability, promise payment, negotiate settlement, or incur defence costs
without Northwind's consent. Liability is a professional decision and is never decided by the
Agent.

## MTR-BEN-01 - Towing and emergency transport

When the insured vehicle cannot be driven safely after an event that may be covered, Northwind may
arrange or reimburse reasonable towing to the nearest suitable repairer or secure place. Reasonable
emergency transport may also be considered up to the limit shown in the schedule. Non-urgent costs
should be agreed with Northwind before they are incurred.

## MTR-BEN-02 - Keys and locks

If vehicle keys are accidentally lost, damaged, or stolen, reasonable replacement, recoding, or
lock costs may be considered up to the schedule limit. A theft report or other evidence may be
required. Any special excess or endorsement in the schedule applies.

## MTR-CLM-01 - What to do after an incident

The claimant should first protect people from immediate danger and seek emergency assistance when
needed. As soon as reasonably possible, the claimant should:

1. prevent avoidable further loss where it is safe to do so;
2. record what happened, when it happened, and where it happened;
3. obtain the other driver's name, contact details, registration, and insurer when another vehicle
   is involved;
4. record witness details when available;
5. take photographs when safe and practical;
6. report theft, unlawful taking, or another legally reportable event to Police; and
7. notify Northwind and retain relevant documents and damaged property for inspection.

Evidence may be supplied later when it is unavailable at FNOL. A pending Police reference must not
erase the report or block unrelated safe work.

## MTR-CLM-02 - Information and cooperation

The claimant must provide honest and reasonably complete information, forward relevant demands or
communications, permit reasonable inspection, and cooperate with authorised assessment or recovery
steps. A missing item should be recorded as missing or pending rather than guessed.

## MTR-EXC-01 - Excess amounts and not-at-fault claims

The base excess and any driver, age, use, or other additional excess come only from the matching
policy schedule. An excess normally applies to each event. The Agent may explain the recorded
amount but must not invent, waive, combine, or decide the final excess. If several excesses or
events may apply, staff review is required.

Being not at fault does not automatically remove an excess. Staff may waive or refund an excess
only when the schedule permits it and the responsible party and recovery conditions are confirmed.
Weather damage, including hail to a parked vehicle, does not itself qualify for a waiver.

## MTR-LIM-01 - Maximum payment

Payment for loss to the insured vehicle cannot exceed the applicable agreed value or market-value
basis recorded in the schedule, after applicable excesses and other policy deductions. Third-party
liability and additional benefits use their own schedule limits. These are ceilings, not promised
payments.

## MTR-SET-01 - Repairs, write-offs and total-loss settlement

After assessing the claim, Northwind may choose to repair the vehicle, replace it with a reasonably
equivalent vehicle, pay the reasonable repair cost, or settle a total loss using the valuation basis
in the schedule. Existing damage, wear, betterment, salvage, finance interests, and unrepaired
damage may affect settlement. The Agent must describe these as possible settlement methods, not a
decision on the current claim.

A write-off means staff determine that repair is uneconomic or unsafe after assessment. The
schedule's agreed-value or market-value basis, excess and finance interests determine the settlement
calculation; a repair estimate alone does not establish a write-off or promised payment.

## MTR-EXC-02 - Driver licence, permission, and restrictions

There is no cover when the vehicle is driven without the owner's permission, by a person who is not
legally licensed for that vehicle, or by a driver excluded by the schedule, unless an approved
exception applies. Named-driver and age restrictions and additional excesses are determined by the
schedule. Unclear driver status requires staff review.

## MTR-EXC-03 - Alcohol, drugs, and unlawful conduct

There is no cover for loss arising while the driver is over the legal alcohol limit, is impaired by
an unlawful drug, intentionally leaves an accident scene contrary to law, or intentionally uses the
vehicle in a criminal act. This section is applied by authorised staff using reliable evidence; the
Agent must not accuse the claimant or make a finding.

## MTR-EXC-04 - Unsafe or unauthorised use

There is no cover for racing, speed testing, hire for reward, or another use excluded by the
schedule. Loss materially caused by an unsafe or unroadworthy condition is excluded unless the
policyholder and driver could not reasonably have known of the condition and had taken reasonable
care. Applicability requires professional review.

## MTR-EXC-05 - Wear, breakdown, tyres, and existing damage

The policy does not cover ordinary wear and tear, deterioration, corrosion, existing damage, or
mechanical or electrical breakdown. Resulting accidental loss may require separate assessment.
Tyre damage from normal braking, puncture, cutting, or bursting is excluded unless it occurs as part
of another covered accident.

## MTR-EXC-06 - Intentional loss and general exclusions

The policy does not cover intentional or reckless loss caused by an insured person, fines or
penalties, punitive damages, nuclear risk, war, terrorism, or a payment prohibited by sanctions.

## MTR-AUTH-01 - Agent and staff authority

The Agent may explain this wording, collect FNOL facts, identify missing schedule or evidence data,
and cite exact sections. It must transfer coverage applicability, liability, claim approval or
rejection, disputed excess, and payment decisions to authorised staff.

## MTR-BEN-03 - Hire car, rental car and alternative transport while your car is repaired

If Northwind accepts a claim under MTR-COV-01, including theft, and the vehicle cannot be driven
legally or safely, is being repaired by an approved repairer, or is stolen and unrecovered,
Northwind may arrange a same-or-smaller-class hire car or reimburse reasonable taxis, ride-share
or public transport. This benefit applies only when the schedule lists `alternative_transport`.

- Limit: NZD 60 a day for up to 21 days per event.
- Starts when the claim is accepted; for theft, after reporting to Police and Northwind.
- Ends when repairs finish, the recovered vehicle is drivable, or 7 days after a total-loss
  settlement offer, whichever is first; the 21-day limit also applies.
- Fuel, tolls, parking, fines, hire-company excess and damage charges are excluded, as are periods
  when the vehicle remains legally and safely drivable.
- No excess applies to this benefit, whoever was at fault. Northwind may recover from a third party.
- Agree hire with Northwind first. Reimbursement uses the same daily limit.

The Agent may explain these conditions, but only staff decide whether they apply to the claim.

## MTR-BEN-04 - Windscreen, window glass and glass excess

Accidental breakage of the insured vehicle's windscreen or window glass is considered under
MTR-COV-01. Repair or replacement must be agreed with Northwind. The applicable excess and any
glass-specific benefit or limit come only from the schedule; this wording does not provide an
automatic excess waiver. Damage to panels or other parts in the same accident remains subject to
ordinary claim terms. Do not drive if broken glass makes the vehicle unsafe or unlawful to drive.

## MTR-COV-03 - Damage caused by an uninsured or unidentified driver

Another driver's lack of insurance does not itself prevent assessment of accidental damage to
the insured vehicle under MTR-COV-01. Record available names, contacts, registration, witnesses
and Police references without guessing missing details. Northwind staff determine liability and
recovery. This section provides no separate uninsured-driver payment or automatic excess waiver;
MTR-EXC-01 and the matching schedule govern the excess.

## MTR-BEN-05 - No-claims discount after an accident

This policy wording does not guarantee protection of a no-claims discount or an unchanged renewal
premium, including after an accident the claimant says was not their fault. Any discount protection
must be expressly recorded in the schedule or endorsement. Staff must confirm its conditions and
the renewal impact; the Agent must not promise that a claim will leave a discount unchanged.

## MTR-COV-04 - Roof boxes, roof racks, accessories and replacement wheels

Fitted accessories, roof racks, roof boxes and replacement wheels are considered with accidental
vehicle loss only where the matching schedule includes them in the insured vehicle or separately
lists them. Declared modifications, accessory limits and the schedule's valuation basis apply.
Receipts may support ownership and value but do not prove insurance. Loose personal possessions
require their own contents wording and schedule. No separate accessory benefit or excess waiver
is granted by this section; uncertain classification requires staff review.
