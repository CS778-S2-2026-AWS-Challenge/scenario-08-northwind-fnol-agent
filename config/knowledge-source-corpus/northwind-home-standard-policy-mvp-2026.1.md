# Northwind Home Standard Policy

This is synthetic wording for an MVP demonstration. It is not a real insurance contract. It must
be read with the matching Northwind policy schedule. The schedule identifies the insured address,
occupancy, sum insured, excesses, limits, optional benefits, endorsements, and period of insurance.
The Agent must not apply this wording when the schedule, version, address, product, or effective
period does not match.

## HOM-DEF-01 - Key terms

- **Home** means the residential building and domestic structures identified at the insured address
  in the policy schedule.
- **Accident** means a sudden and unintended event.
- **Event** means one occurrence, or a series of occurrences with one originating cause.
- **Loss** means accidental physical damage or destruction.
- **Uninhabitable** means the home cannot reasonably and safely be lived in.
- **Excess** means the part of an accepted claim the policyholder is responsible for, as shown in
  the policy schedule.

Land, contents, vehicles, and commercial property are not part of the home unless a schedule or
endorsement expressly says otherwise.

## HOM-COV-01 - Sudden accidental physical loss

Home Standard includes cover for sudden accidental physical loss to the insured home at the insured
address during the period of insurance. Examples may include fire, storm, flood, impact, escape of
water, and malicious damage, subject to the schedule, exclusions, responsibilities, excesses, and
limits.

This section does not itself confirm coverage for a particular incident. Cause, timing, address,
maintenance, occupancy, exclusions, statutory natural-hazard arrangements, and endorsements may
need professional assessment.

## HOM-BEN-01 - Emergency work

The policy may pay reasonable costs for urgent work needed to prevent immediate further damage or
make the site safe after an event that may be covered. The claimant should obtain Northwind's
agreement before non-urgent permanent repairs. Emergency work should be photographed and receipts
retained where practical.

## HOM-BEN-02 - Temporary accommodation

If covered loss makes an owner-occupied home uninhabitable, reasonable temporary accommodation for
the household and domestic pets may be considered up to the schedule limit and time period. The
benefit ends when the home is reasonably habitable, the policyholder moves to another permanent
home, the claim is settled, or the schedule limit is reached. Entitlement under another policy must
be disclosed.

## HOM-BEN-03 - Hidden gradual water-damage endorsement

Gradual damage is generally excluded. A limited contribution may be considered only when the
matching schedule expressly includes the `hidden_water_damage` endorsement and the damage results
from a hidden leak or overflow from a permanently installed internal water system. The leak must not
have been visible, obvious, known, or reasonably discoverable earlier. The endorsement limit and
special excess come from the schedule.

This benefit is deliberately treated as review-required because the facts and wording conditions
are often material and disputed.

## HOM-NHZ-01 - Natural hazards and statutory coordination

Earthquake, volcanic activity, tsunami, landslide, and specified storm or flood damage may interact
with New Zealand statutory natural-hazard cover. Private policy wording, the schedule, the incident
date, and the applicable statutory source must be checked together. The Agent may explain the
process and identify missing records but must not decide which organisation is liable or how the
loss will be allocated.

## HOM-CLM-01 - What to do after home damage

The claimant should first protect people from immediate danger and follow emergency authority
instructions. As soon as reasonably possible, the claimant should:

1. take safe and reasonable steps to prevent further damage;
2. contact the appropriate emergency service for an active emergency;
3. report theft, burglary, vandalism, or another legally reportable event to Police;
4. notify Northwind and describe what happened, when, where, and what was damaged;
5. retain photographs, videos, receipts, quotes, and relevant communications;
6. keep damaged property available for reasonable inspection unless safety requires disposal; and
7. record any urgent work and its cost.

Evidence that does not yet exist may be marked pending and supplied later.

## HOM-CLM-02 - Information and cooperation

The claimant must provide honest and reasonably complete information and cooperate with authorised
inspection, assessment, repair, and recovery steps. A claimant must not make a settlement agreement
with another party on Northwind's behalf. Unknown information must remain unknown rather than be
estimated as fact.

## HOM-EXC-01 - Excesses and limits

The base excess, natural-hazard excess, unoccupied-home excess, and any endorsement excess come only
from the matching schedule. An excess normally applies to each event. The maximum settlement is
bounded by the home sum insured and any benefit or sublimit in the schedule. These amounts are
ceilings and responsibilities, not promised payments. Multiple-event or multiple-excess questions
require staff review.

## HOM-SET-01 - How a home claim may be settled

After assessment, Northwind may repair, rebuild, replace, pay the reasonable cost of an approved
repair, or pay another amount permitted by the schedule and valuation basis. Settlement may account
for existing damage, depreciation where applicable, betterment, undamaged matching areas, building
standards, professional fees, demolition, salvage, and secured financial interests. The settlement
method is selected by authorised staff, not the Agent.

## HOM-EXC-02 - Wear, maintenance, and gradual causes

The policy does not cover ordinary wear and tear, corrosion, rot, mould, mildew, gradual
deterioration, or damage caused by failure to maintain the home. Section HOM-BEN-03 is the only
synthetic MVP exception for hidden gradual water damage and applies only when the endorsement is in
the schedule.

## HOM-EXC-03 - Defects and faulty work

The policy does not cover the cost of correcting defective design, materials, construction, or
workmanship. Separate resulting accidental damage may require professional assessment and must not
be assumed by the Agent.

## HOM-EXC-04 - Unoccupancy and change of use

The policyholder must tell Northwind about material changes in occupancy or use. Loss while the home
is unoccupied beyond the period in the schedule, rented without approval, used materially for an
undeclared business, or undergoing major unapproved work may be excluded or subject to an additional
excess. Applicability requires the schedule and staff review.

## HOM-EXC-05 - Intentional loss and general exclusions

The policy does not cover intentional or reckless loss caused by an insured person, unlawful
activity materially connected to the loss, fines or penalties, nuclear risk, war, terrorism, or a
payment prohibited by sanctions.

## HOM-AUTH-01 - Agent and staff authority

The Agent may explain this wording, collect FNOL facts, identify missing schedule or evidence data,
and cite exact sections. It must transfer coverage applicability, natural-hazard allocation, claim
approval or rejection, disputed excess, and settlement or payment decisions to authorised staff.
